About

I'm a Senior PM Lead at Microsoft Research based in the San Francisco Bay Area. With a wealth of experience, I specialize in guiding products from concept to fruition and harnessing the power of innovative technologies.

At Microsoft Research, I work at the intersection of large language models, agentic AI, and product strategy — contributing to foundational models like Phi-4-reasoning and Fara-7B. Before that, I spent 3 years at Google as a UX Researcher and Product Manager on the Shopping team, where I led research for products launched at Google I/O 2023.

I hold a degree from Georgia Institute of Technology with coursework in AI, computer vision, deep learning, and ethics. I'm driven by the belief that AI should be both powerful and responsible.

Outside of work, I'm a creative at heart — an avid reader (55 books in 2022!), singer, painter, and baker who experiments with recipes like lavender mint cake.
