About

I'm a technical program leader who has shipped 9 frontier models and agentic systems at Microsoft AI Superintelligence on an 8-week release cadence, and led generative AI strategy at Google Shopping. I run release, evaluation, and safety programs across reasoning models and computer-use agents.

At Microsoft AI, I own the product portfolio for the small language model and agentic AI programs — from Phi through Fara1.5 — and built the org's model development, evaluation, and release pipeline: a single readiness path every model now ships through. That means driving CUA, RL, Responsible AI, Legal, GTM, Sales, Foundry, and Marketing to each launch.

Before Microsoft, I spent nearly three years at Google as Product Strategy & Research Lead on Shopping's generative AI work, defining strategy for the Search Generative Experience and owning the team's genAI roadmap.

I hold an M.S. in Human-Computer Interaction from Georgia Tech and a B.Tech in Chemical Engineering from NIT Karnataka. I'm driven by the belief that frontier AI should be both capable and responsibly released.

Outside of work, I'm a creative at heart — an avid reader (55 books in 2022!), singer, painter, and baker who experiments with recipes like lavender mint cake.
